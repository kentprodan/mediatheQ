# Plan: Mobile 3 · Video

- Status: Accepted (owner decisions 2026-11-12, section 10)
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Milestone: `Mobile 3 · Video` (ADR 0025, order 5; GitHub milestone #4)
- Issue drafts: [mobile3-issues.md](mobile3-issues.md). ADRs: 0047-0051 (Accepted).

## 1. Goal

The phone app plays the owner's Jellyfin **movies and series** next to the music: browse Movies and Series in the
Library, open a movie or an episode, play it full screen in the right orientation with the audio track and
subtitles of choice, resume where the server says, see "Continue watching" and "Next up" on Home, keep watching in
picture-in-picture, and download a movie or a season for a flight. Music keeps working exactly as today: one
player, one service, one notification; a video takes the player over and hands the music queue back when it ends.
An **app mode** (Music, Video or Both; ADR 0051) chosen in onboarding, in Settings or on Home decides which of the
two the app shows: Music is today's app, Video hides every music surface, Both shows both.

Exit criterion (last issue): a full movie and a full season episode by episode played on the Pixel 9 (Wi-Fi and
mobile data, with subtitles, in PiP, one of them downloaded), resume points agree with the Jellyfin web UI, and the
owner used the app for a week of watching.

## 3. Research summary (official sources, checked 2026-11-12)

### Jellyfin 12 (OpenAPI 12.3.0 copy in `provider/jellyfin/api/openapi-12.3.0.json`)

- **Item types** (`BaseItemKind`): `Movie`, `Series`, `Season`, `Episode` (also `Video`, `BoxSet`, `Trailer`, `MusicVideo`, not in scope). Libraries are `CollectionType` `movies` / `tvshows`; `GET /Items?includeItemTypes=<kind>&recursive=true` with `startIndex` / `limit` / `sortBy` pages the whole server like the music sync (R7, R8 apply: explicit `includeItemTypes` + `recursive`, exact query pinned by a contract test). Episode fields: `SeriesId`, `SeriesName`, `SeasonId`, `SeasonName`, `IndexNumber` (episode), `ParentIndexNumber` (season), `SeriesPrimaryImageTag`, `ParentBackdropItemId` / `ParentBackdropImageTags`, `ParentThumbItemId`, `SeriesThumbImageTag`. Series: `Status` (`Continuing` / `Ended`), `ChildCount`, `RecursiveItemCount`, `EndDate`. Common: `RunTimeTicks`, `OfficialRating`, `CommunityRating`, `CriticRating`, `Taglines`, `Overview`, `ProductionYear`, `PremiereDate`, `GenreItems`, `Studios`, `People`, `Width`, `Height`, `IsHD`, `HasSubtitles`, `VideoType`, `Container`, `CanDownload`, `ImageTags` (`Primary`, `Thumb`, `Backdrop`, `Logo`, `Banner`), `BackdropImageTags`, `ImageBlurHashes`.
- **`ItemFields`** worth asking for: `MediaSources` (brings `MediaStreams` and `Size`, R19), `Chapters`, `Trickplay`, `Overview`, `Genres`, `Studios`, `People`, `DateCreated`, `SortName`, `Taglines`, `ProviderIds`, `Width`, `Height`, `IsHD`, `SeriesStudio`, `SeasonUserData`.
- **`UserItemDataDto`**: `PlaybackPositionTicks`, `PlayedPercentage`, `Played`, `PlayCount`, `LastPlayedDate`, `IsFavorite`, `Rating`, `UnplayedItemCount` (series and seasons). Unlike music (R20) the server **does** keep video positions: `POST /Sessions/Playing/Progress` and `/Stopped` with `PositionTicks` write them (the server's defaults keep a position only for items longer than its minimum resume duration and between its minimum and maximum resume percentages; to confirm on 12.3.0 with a fixture). Watched: `POST /UserPlayedItems/{itemId}` (`datePlayed`) and `DELETE /UserPlayedItems/{itemId}`; `UserDataChanged` socket messages already reach `LiveSync`.
- **Resume and Next up**: `GET /UserItems/Resume` (`userId`, `mediaTypes=Video`, `includeItemTypes=Movie,Episode`, `fields`, `enableImageTypes`, `limit`, `excludeActiveSessions`) and `GET /Shows/NextUp` (`userId`, `seriesId`, `fields`, `enableResumable`, `enableRewatching`, `nextUpDateCutoff`, `limit`). `GET /Shows/{seriesId}/Seasons` and `GET /Shows/{seriesId}/Episodes` (`seasonId`, `isMissing`, `adjacentTo`) exist, but the sync reads seasons and episodes through `/Items` pages, so one pager and one staging path serve music and video alike; the Shows routes are used only for `NextUp`.
- **`PlaybackInfo` for video**: `POST /Items/{itemId}/PlaybackInfo` with `PlaybackInfoDto { UserId, MaxStreamingBitrate, StartTimeTicks, AudioStreamIndex, SubtitleStreamIndex, MaxAudioChannels, MediaSourceId, DeviceProfile, EnableDirectPlay, EnableDirectStream, EnableTranscoding, AllowVideoStreamCopy, AllowAudioStreamCopy, AlwaysBurnInSubtitleWhenTranscoding }`. The answer's `MediaSourceInfo` has `SupportsDirectPlay`, `SupportsDirectStream` (remux: video copied, container or audio changed), `SupportsTranscoding`, `TranscodingUrl`, `TranscodingSubProtocol` (`http` | `hls`), `TranscodingContainer`, `DefaultAudioStreamIndex`, `DefaultSubtitleStreamIndex`, `RunTimeTicks`, `Bitrate`, `Size`, `Container`, `HasSegments`, and `MediaStreams[]`. The direct-play URL is `/Videos/{itemId}/stream?static=true&mediaSourceId=&playSessionId=` (`GET` and `HEAD`; no security entry in the OpenAPI, like images, but the app sends the header as for music); the transcoding URL comes from the server and carries `ApiKey` (R10: stripped, header sent).
- **`DeviceProfile` for video**: `DirectPlayProfile { Type: Video, Container, VideoCodec, AudioCodec }`; `TranscodingProfile { Type: Video, Container, VideoCodec, AudioCodec, Protocol: hls, Context: Streaming, MaxAudioChannels, MinSegments, SegmentLength, BreakOnNonKeyFrames, CopyTimestamps, EnableSubtitlesInManifest, Conditions }`; `CodecProfile { Type: Video | VideoAudio | Audio, Codec, Container, Conditions[], ApplyConditions[] }` with `ProfileCondition { Condition: Equals | NotEquals | LessThanEqual | GreaterThanEqual | EqualsAny, Property, Value, IsRequired }` over `ProfileConditionValue` (`Width`, `Height`, `VideoBitDepth`, `VideoBitrate`, `VideoFramerate`, `VideoLevel`, `VideoProfile`, `VideoRangeType`, `VideoCodecTag`, `AudioChannels`, `AudioBitrate`, `IsSecondaryAudio`, `NumAudioStreams`, ...); `SubtitleProfile { Format, Method: Encode | Embed | External | Hls | Drop, Language, Container }`; `MaxStaticBitrate` caps direct play, `MaxStreamingBitrate` caps everything.
- **Subtitles**: a `MediaStream` of `Type: Subtitle` has `Codec` (`subrip`, `webvtt`, `ass`, `ssa`, `pgssub`, `dvdsub`, `mov_text`, ...), `Language`, `DisplayTitle`, `Title`, `IsDefault`, `IsForced`, `IsHearingImpaired`, `IsExternal`, `IsTextSubtitleStream`, `SupportsExternalStream`, and after `PlaybackInfo` the chosen `DeliveryMethod` and `DeliveryUrl`. External delivery: `GET /Videos/{routeItemId}/{routeMediaSourceId}/Subtitles/{routeIndex}/Stream.{routeFormat}` (`format` `vtt` / `srt` / `ass` ..., optional `startPositionTicks`, `addVttTimeMap`; no security entry), HLS delivery `GET /Videos/{itemId}/{mediaSourceId}/Subtitles/{index}/subtitles.m3u8` (authenticated). Image subtitles (PGS, DVD) cannot be delivered as text: direct play keeps them embedded, a transcode burns them in (`Encode`). Jellyfin's codec page: WebVTT is what HLS streams carry; burn-in is the most expensive path ([codec support](https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/clients/codec-support/)).
- **Chapters and trickplay**: `fields=Chapters` -> `ChapterInfo { StartPositionTicks, Name, ImageTag }` (image `/Items/{id}/Images/Chapter/{index}?tag=`); `fields=Trickplay` -> `Trickplay: { <mediaSourceId>: { <width>: TrickplayInfoDto { Width, Height, TileWidth, TileHeight, ThumbnailCount, Interval (ms), Bandwidth } } }`, tiles `GET /Videos/{itemId}/Trickplay/{width}/{index}.jpg` (authenticated; the tile index is `thumbnail / (TileWidth * TileHeight)`, the cell the remainder), playlist `/Videos/{itemId}/Trickplay/{width}/tiles.m3u8`. Media segments (intro / outro skip): `GET /MediaSegments/{itemId}?includeSegmentTypes=` -> `MediaSegmentDto { Id, ItemId, Type: Intro | Outro | Recap | Preview | Commercial | Unknown, StartTicks, EndTicks }`.
- **Intro Skipper plugin** ([intro-skipper/intro-skipper](https://github.com/intro-skipper/intro-skipper), checked 2026-11-12): requires Jellyfin 12.0.0 or newer and "does NOT modify the UI" since 10.10; its wiki says the plugin database is the source of truth and "Jellyfin's native MediaSegments table is a synchronized mirror used by playback clients"; it detects introductions, credits (endings), previews, recaps and commercials (movies: chapter and black-frame analysis, credits in a 15-minute window), and its General settings can request automatic skipping of intros and credits, with a client's own Jellyfin playback preference taking precedence. The owner's server already runs it: the OpenAPI 12.3.0 copy lists its routes (`/Episode/{itemId}/Segments` -> `SegmentDto { Id, Type, Start, End, Source, Suppressed }`, `/Intros/*`, `/IntroSkipper`, `/MediaSegmentsApi`, `/SkipButtonCss/*`) and **no** legacy `/Episode/{id}/IntroTimestamps` route, so the app reads Jellyfin's `/MediaSegments/{itemId}` only, with no plugin-specific or legacy endpoint. Which `MediaSegmentType` the plugin's "Credits" become (expected `Outro`) is confirmed by the recorded fixture and written as an R line.
- **HDR metadata**: `MediaStream.VideoRange` (`SDR` | `HDR`), `VideoRangeType` (`SDR`, `HDR10`, `HLG`, `DOVI`, `DOVIWithHDR10`, `DOVIWithHLG`, `DOVIWithSDR`, `DOVIWithEL`, `DOVIWithHDR10Plus`, `DOVIWithELHDR10Plus`, `DOVIInvalid`, `HDR10Plus`), `VideoDoViTitle`, `DvProfile`, `DvBlSignalCompatibilityId`, `Hdr10PlusPresentFlag`, `ColorTransfer`, `ColorPrimaries`, `BitDepth`, `Profile`, `Level`, `AverageFrameRate`, `RealFrameRate`, `ReferenceFrameRate`, `Rotation`.
- **Reports**: `PlaybackStartInfo` / `PlaybackProgressInfo` (21 fields) take `AudioStreamIndex`, `SubtitleStreamIndex`, `PlayMethod`, `PositionTicks`, `IsPaused`, `CanSeek` next to the ids the app already sends (jellyfin skill, section 6).

### Media3 (releases page, checked 2026-11-12)

- Stable is still **1.11.1** (2026-09-10); **1.12.0-beta01** shipped 2026-11-11 (alpha01 2026-10-21, alpha02 2026-10-26). 1.12 adds to `media3-ui-compose`: `CuesState` / `rememberCuesState` (alpha01), `PresentationState.videoAspectRatio` replacing `videoSizeDp`, `alignment` on `ContentFrame` / `Modifier.resizeWithContentScale` (alpha01), a `subtitles` slot in the material3 `Player` / `ContentFrame` with text and bitmap cue rendering (beta01); and for video: an H.265 decoder fallback for Dolby Vision profile 7 and a `MediaCodecUtil` fix that no longer picks a decoder that does not support the format's profile ([#3486]). We stay on **1.11.1** and write the plan against it; the 1.12 bump is one issue after it is stable (V11 lists what it simplifies). Source: [media3 releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/media3).
- `media3-ui-compose` 1.11.1: `PlayerSurface(player, modifier, surfaceType)` with `SURFACE_TYPE_SURFACE_VIEW` / `SURFACE_TYPE_TEXTURE_VIEW`, `ContentFrame`, `Modifier.resizeWithContentScale`, and state holders `PresentationState` (`coverSurface` until `EVENT_RENDERED_FIRST_FRAME`, `showArtwork`, `videoAspectRatio`), `PlayPauseButtonState`, `SeekBackButtonState`, `SeekForwardButtonState`, `CurrentMediaItemState`, `PlaylistState`, `ErrorState`; all `@UnstableApi`. `media3-ui-compose-material3` (the `Player` / `MiniController` composables) depends on `com.google.android.material` and plain Material 3, not Expressive, so it is not used. Sources: [Media3 UI in Compose](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/ui/compose), [PresentationState](https://developer.android.com/reference/kotlin/androidx/media3/ui/compose/state/PresentationState).
- **Surface**: `SurfaceView` over `TextureView`: lower power (TextureView up to +30 %), accurate frame timing, "higher quality HDR video output on capable devices", secure output; `PlayerSurface` links the surface to the player lifecycle-aware. Sources: [Surfaces](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/ui/surface), [Battery consumption](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/battery-consumption).
- **Subtitles in 1.11.1**: no Compose cue composable yet (`CuesState` is 1.12). Cues arrive through `Player.Listener.onCues(CueGroup)` / `player.currentCues`; `androidx.media3.ui.SubtitleView` renders text and bitmap cues and follows the system caption style (`CaptioningManager`). Formats: standalone WebVTT, TTML, SubRip, SSA/ASS (`MimeTypes.TEXT_VTT`, `APPLICATION_TTML`, `APPLICATION_SUBRIP`, `TEXT_SSA`) side-loaded with `MediaItem.SubtitleConfiguration(uri, mimeType, language, selectionFlags, label)` (the factory merges them with a `MergingMediaSource`; "only supported with `DefaultMediaSourceFactory`"); embedded in MP4 / Matroska / WebM through the extractors; in HLS WebVTT, CEA-608 / 708. Sources: [Supported formats](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/supported-formats), [Media items](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/media-items).
- **Containers and codecs**: progressive MP4, FMP4, Matroska, WebM, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS; HLS with MPEG-TS and FMP4 / CMAF; sample formats come from the platform decoders (`MediaCodecList`), so the DeviceProfile is built from them (media3 skill, rule 1). ExoPlayer extracts Dolby Vision in MP4 and HDR10+ in Matroska / WebM; "decoding and displaying HDR content depends on support from the Android platform and device"; the default decoder selector picks a decoder that supports the stream's profile even if it is lower in the codec list.
- **Track selection**: `TrackSelectionParameters.Builder.setPreferredAudioLanguage`, `setPreferredTextLanguage`, `setTrackTypeDisabled(C.TRACK_TYPE_TEXT, true)`, `setOverrideForType(TrackSelectionOverride(group.mediaTrackGroup, index))`; tracks from `Player.getCurrentTracks()` / `onTracksChanged`. Source: [Track selection](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/track-selection).
- **Downloads**: a progressive file is one `DownloadRequest(contentId, uri)` (today's path); adaptive streams need `DownloadHelper` with track selection and `streamKeys` on the `MediaItem`. Source: [Downloading media](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/downloading-media).
- **HLS**: `media3-exoplayer-hls` (already a dependency, ADR 0019); multivariant playlists adapt by bandwidth; chunkless preparation needs `CODECS` in `#EXT-X-STREAM-INF`. Source: [HLS](https://developer.android.com/media/media3/exoplayer/hls).

## 5. Playback design (ADR 0047, 0050, short form)

1. **One player.** A video is a one-item playlist on the session's `ExoPlayer` (plus the next episode appended when auto-play is on). Starting a video saves the music queue's place (`QueuePersistence` already saves on every change; a video item is never saved as the queue), the Now Playing overlay collapses, the notification shows the video. When the video item ends, is cleared, or the user leaves the player with Stop, the service restores the saved music queue paused (the cold-start restore path). Media buttons, audio focus, output profiles, EQ, offload rules apply to the video's audio unchanged (passthrough comes with TV 2).
2. **The surface** is attached by the video screen through the `MediaController` (`PlayerSurface(controller, surfaceType = SURFACE_TYPE_SURFACE_VIEW)`; the controller forwards `COMMAND_SET_VIDEO_SURFACE` to the service's player in the same process). Leaving the screen clears the surface; playback continues only in PiP or as audio when the setting says so (default: pause when the screen is left without PiP).
3. **Stream selection** (`StreamResolver.resolveVideo`, pure `DeviceProfileBuilder.video`): direct play of what the phone decodes (containers `mp4`, `m4v`, `mkv`, `webm`, `ts`; video codecs from `MediaCodecList`: `h264`, `hevc`, `av1`, `vp9`, `dvhe`/`dvh1` only with a `video/dolby-vision` decoder; audio codecs as for music with multichannel allowed: `aac`, `ac3`, `eac3` (with the JOC rule of #242), `flac`, `opus`, `vorbis`, `mp3`, `pcm`; `truehd` / `dts` never on the phone); `CodecProfile` conditions: `VideoRangeType EqualsAny` the ranges the display and decoder support (always `SDR`; `HDR10`, `HLG`, `HDR10Plus` when the display lists them with a Main10 decoder; `DOVI*` only with the decoder and `HDR_TYPE_DOLBY_VISION`), `Width <= 3840`, `VideoBitDepth <= 10`, `AudioChannels <= 8`; `MaxStreamingBitrate` and a `Height LessThanEqual` condition from the video quality setting (`VideoQuality(maxHeight, bitrate)`: Original, 1080p / 10 Mbps, 720p / 4 Mbps, 480p / 1.5 Mbps, 360p / 0.7 Mbps; defaults Original on unmetered and **480p** on metered; the bitrate ladder is the plan's, V7 may align it with Jellyfin web's quality menu). Remux (`SupportsDirectStream`) is accepted as it is. Transcodes are **HLS fMP4** (`TranscodingProfile Type=Video, Container=mp4, Protocol=hls, VideoCodec=hevc,h264, AudioCodec=aac,opus`, `MaxAudioChannels` 6, `EnableSubtitlesInManifest` false, `BreakOnNonKeyFrames` true) played by the existing `LazyStreamMediaSource` HLS child (ADR 0019); the server tone-maps HDR it may not send.
4. **Subtitles**: `SubtitleProfile`s `External` for `subrip`, `webvtt`, `ass`, `ssa`, `mov_text` (served as `.vtt`), `Embed` for the same text codecs and `pgssub` (Media3 reads PGS in Matroska), `Hls` for `webvtt` on transcodes, `Encode` (burn-in) for `pgssub` / `dvdsub` on transcodes. The app passes `SubtitleStreamIndex` so the server decides; `MediaStream.DeliveryMethod` / `DeliveryUrl` of the answer become `SubtitleDelivery` entries: external ones are side-loaded as `SubtitleConfiguration` (through the auth-header data source, token-free URLs), embedded ones are Media3 text tracks selected by `TrackSelectionOverride`, burned-in ones show as "Subtitles burned in" with no track to pick. Default choice per Settings -> Video: Off / **Forced only** (default) / Preferred language (`setPreferredTextLanguage`, `setSelectUndeterminedTextLanguage`); audio: preferred language, else the server default index. A choice made in the player, on the movie page, on an episode row or for a whole season is remembered in `video_track_choice` (item id or season id; indexes plus languages, so a season choice carries to episodes with other stream orders) and wins over the defaults: item > season > Settings. Changing a track that needs a new server stream (burn-in, or a transcode's audio) resolves again with a new play session at the same position (the `InstantTranscoder` replace path).
5. **Rendering cues**: `MqSubtitles` wraps `androidx.media3.ui.SubtitleView` in an `AndroidView`, fed by `onCues` through `PlaybackConnection.videoState.cues`; it follows the system caption style and renders bitmap cues. When Media3 1.12 is stable, `rememberCuesState` replaces the listener (one wrapper changes).
6. **Resume and reporting**: positions are the server's. `playVideo` starts at `userData.playbackPosition` (Resume) or 0 (Play from start); reports go through today's `PlaybackReporter` every 10 s with `AudioStreamIndex` / `SubtitleStreamIndex`; the stopped report writes the final position; the socket's `UserDataChanged` updates Room; `continueWatching` and `nextUp` refresh from the server when Home opens. No app-side resume table for video.
7. **PiP**: the player screen asks the activity's `PipController` to enable auto-enter with the surface's bounds as the source rect while a video plays; in PiP only `PlayerSurface` and `MqSubtitles` are composed; leaving PiP returns to the player screen. Actions: play / pause, next episode.
8. **Downloads**: originals only (`DeviceProfileBuilder.videoDownload` = the Original direct-play lists with no transcoding profile; `PlaybackInfo` answering a transcode fails the download with "This video cannot be downloaded as it is"); a movie, a single episode or a season (no series download); external text subtitles are fetched beside the file; downloaded videos play offline with embedded and downloaded subtitles; Downloads tab groups Movies and Seasons (#168); offline mode lists only downloaded videos (ADR 0021 parameters).
9. **Skipping** (Intro Skipper segments through `/MediaSegments`): a pure `segmentAction(position, segments, preferences, alreadySkipped)` shows "Skip intro" / "Skip recap" / "Skip credits" at a segment's start (hidden after 8 s unless the controls are up, never twice per segment) or seeks past it by itself, per Settings -> Video -> Skipping (Off / Show button / Skip automatically, per kind; default Show button); skipping credits starts the next-episode countdown when auto-play is on.

## 6. How the music app and the video player coexist

**App mode (ADR 0051).** `AppMode` is one app-wide setting (not per account): `Music` is today's app with no
video surface anywhere and no video sync steps; `Video` shows the video library, Home, Search and Downloads with
no music surface, no music sync steps, no Now Playing overlay and a mini player only while a video plays (the saved
music queue stays saved for a switch back and is not restored at start); `Both` shows music with video placed as
the table below says. It is asked once in onboarding after the first sign-in (Both preselected when the server has
video libraries; skipped as Music when `capabilities.video` is false), changed in Settings -> Interface -> App mode
and with a segmented toggle at the top of Home; existing installs read as Music. Switching runs a sync for a kind
never synced on the account. Backup (#150) carries it like every preference. A Subsonic account behaves as Music
whatever the setting.

| Place | Music today | With video (Both; Video mode drops the music column) |
|---|---|---|
| Library hub (ADR 0028) | tiles Albums, Artists, Genres, Tracks, Playlists, Years, Labels | plus Movies and Series tiles (hidden for an account without `capabilities.video` or with zero items); `LibraryShortcut.Movies / Series` |
| Navigation entries (#122) | Home, Library, Albums, ..., Search, Downloads | `MainNavEntry.Movies`, `Series` as optional entries (`TopLevelTab`), off by default; music entries only with `mode.music`, video entries only with `mode.video` (Video mode defaults: Home, Library, Search, Downloads) |
| Home (#127) | sections of albums / artists / tracks / playlists | the mode toggle (Music / Video / Both) at the top; sections Continue watching (poster + progress line), Next up (16:9 thumbs), Recently added movies, Recently added episodes; off by default except Continue watching and Next up; gear settings as today |
| Search (#136) | albums, artists, tracks, playlists | groups Movies, Series, Episodes from the same FTS table; the filter chips gain Video |
| Downloads (#168) | collections by type | Movies and Seasons collections (single episodes under their season), episode rows with sizes; "Converted copy" never shows for video; a download of a kind outside the mode is kept and counted, not listed |
| Now Playing overlay + mini player | the music queue | while a video item plays: the mini player shows poster, title ("S2 E4 · Title"), play / pause, and opens the video player; swipe-to-skip is off; the overlay stays closed; when the music queue is restored the overlay is back |
| Playback service, notification, buttons, focus, output profiles | music | unchanged; video audio obeys the same `AudioPolicy`; the notification shows the poster |
| Settings -> Interface | ... | **App mode** page (Music / Video / Both with one sentence each); in Video mode the music-only pages are hidden (Decoding and transcoding, Transitions, Bluetooth lyrics, Now playing screens, Album / Artist / Genre / Playlist page settings); Output settings, Equalizer, Audio focus and Headset buttons stay, since they apply to video audio |

| Accounts (#147) | one database per account | video tables in the same database; a Subsonic account (Servers 1) has `video = false` and shows nothing |

## 7. Data model and Room changes

- Schema **11 -> 12**: `series`, `season`, `video`, `video_stream` and the four `_staging` twins; indexes `video(server_id, series_id)`, `video(server_id, season_id)`, `video(server_id, kind, sort_name)`, `video_stream(server_id, video_id)`. All new tables: `AutoMigration(from = 11, to = 12)` is enough (no column changes), with the migration test the testing skill asks for (section 5) and the exported `12.json`.
- `video` columns: `server_id`, `id`, `kind` (`movie` | `episode`), `title`, `sort_name`, `overview`, `tagline`, `year`, `premiere_date`, `official_rating`, `community_rating`, `runtime_ms`, `date_added`, `series_id`, `series_title`, `season_id`, `season_number`, `episode_number`, `container`, `size_bytes`, `video_codec`, `width`, `height`, `frame_rate`, `bit_depth`, `video_range_type`, `dovi_profile`, `has_subtitles`, embedded `image` (Primary), `backdrop`, `thumb` columns (`ImageColumns`), embedded `userData` (`UserDataColumns`) plus `position_ms`, `played_percentage`.
- `video_stream`: `server_id`, `video_id`, `index`, `type` (`audio` | `subtitle`), `codec`, `language`, `title`, `display_title`, `is_default`, `is_forced`, `is_hearing_impaired`, `is_external`, `is_text`, `channels`, `channel_layout`. Video streams of type `Video` fold into the `video` row (one video stream per file is the rule; a second one is ignored).
- Sync: after the music steps, `movies` -> `series` -> `seasons` -> `episodes` pages (500 each, `fields=MediaSources,Overview,Genres,Studios,DateCreated,SortName,Taglines`, `enableImageTypes=Primary,Backdrop,Thumb`), into staging, one swap; each kind only when the app mode includes it (ADR 0051). Genres of movies and series are stored as names in a text column (`genres`, comma-separated); a genre page for video is later (owner decision 11).
- Schema **12 -> 13** (V5): `video_track_choice(server_id, item_id, audio_index, audio_language, subtitle_index, subtitle_language, subtitle_off)`, `item_id` a movie, episode or season id; no foreign keys (a sync keeps it), cleared on sign-out like `album_resume`; `AutoMigration(12 -> 13)` with its test. The app mode itself is a DataStore preference, not a table.
- Chapters, trickplay info and media segments are **not** stored: they are read from the server when the player opens (`fields=Chapters,Trickplay` on a single `/Items?ids=` request; `/MediaSegments/{id}`), cached in memory for the session. Offline: no chapters, no trickplay, no segments (the player hides them).
- FTS: `search` rows for movies (`title`, `year`), series (`title`), episodes (`title`, `series title`), kind column extended.
- Nothing changes for music tables; the sync's music steps and tests are untouched.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Why | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Library size: a big series library breaks the "< 10k items" assumption behind the full local copy and the offline id list (ADR 0021) | episodes count like tracks | open question 1 asks for the owner's counts; the sync pages 500 at a time and the episode page requests `MediaSources` only (no `People`); if the library is > 10k items, episodes sync lazily per series (one ADR) |
| `MediaController.setVideoSurfaceView` through the session | the surface crosses the controller API | the controller and the service live in one process (no `android:process`); a device test asserts the first frame renders through the controller; fallback: the activity binds the service directly for the surface (ADR 0047 names it) |
| HDR / Dolby Vision on the Pixel 9 | Google's page says only "HDR support"; DV profiles 5 / 7 / 8 differ; Media3 1.11.1 has no P7 fallback | the profile declares DV only with a real `video/dolby-vision` decoder and `HDR_TYPE_DOLBY_VISION`; otherwise the server tone-maps (transcode); V7 records the phone's decoders and display types in the skill; a wrong declaration shows as a server transcode, never a failed play |
| Transcode seeks and start-up | HLS transcodes seek by segment (as music, R15) but video segments are heavier | `BreakOnNonKeyFrames` true, `MinSegments` 1, `StartTimeTicks` for Resume so the server starts there; a slow-start message exists (`SlowStarts`) |
| Subtitle timing on a transcode with external subtitles | the HLS stream starts at `StartTimeTicks`, the side-loaded VTT at 0 | ask the external subtitle with `startPositionTicks` matching the transcode start (the route takes it), or prefer `Hls` delivery on transcodes (`subtitles.m3u8`); contract fixture for both; the issue picks after a device test |
| ASS/SSA styling | Media3 renders SSA with basic styling; Jellyfin converts `ass` to `vtt` on external delivery and loses styling | accept; the "burn in styled subtitles" switch (off by default) in Settings -> Video asks for `Encode` for `ass` / `ssa` |
| PiP and the single activity | PiP applies to `MainActivity`, so the whole app is in the window | only the surface and cues compose in PiP mode (`rememberIsInPipMode`); leaving PiP lands on the player screen; Back from the player while playing enters PiP only when the setting says so |
| Music queue hand-back | a video replaces the playlist; the saved queue must survive | `QueuePersistence` ignores video items (the saved queue and its flag stay as before the video); a Robolectric test: queue -> video -> end -> restored paused at the same entry and position |
| Video audio through the music `AudioPolicy` | offload and EQ were tuned for music | offload is per route and already off with EQ; `AudioPolicy.offload` gets a `video` input that disables offload while a video plays (A/V sync with offload is not guaranteed); one rule, one test |
| Screenshot tests of a screen with a SurfaceView | layoutlib cannot render a surface | the player screen previews use a `Box` stand-in for `PlayerSurface` (the stateless `VideoPlayerScreen` takes the surface as a slot) |
| Two kinds of items in one `LibrarySource` / sync | more provider code | the pager and staging are shared; each new request shape has its contract test; Subsonic answers `MqError.Client(0, "unsupported")` and is never called (`capabilities.video = false`) |
| App mode gates spread over many screens | a missed gate shows a music tile in Video mode or a video row in Music mode | one `AppMode` flow from `:core:data`, read by ViewModels into `UiState` (never a per-screen preference); `HomeSections`, `LibraryShortcut`, `MainNavEntry` and the Downloads grouping filter in pure functions with tests per mode; the V2 Robolectric test walks Home, Library, Search, Downloads and the Settings hub in each mode |
| Intro Skipper's segment types | the plugin's "Credits" must map to a Jellyfin `MediaSegmentType` (expected `Outro`); the mirror can lag a scan | the fixture is recorded from an analyzed episode and pins the mapping (R line); segments are read when the player opens, not stored, so a new scan shows at the next play |

## 9. Decisions (recommended option first; all accepted by the owner on 2026-11-12, see section 10)

1. **Video plays in the session's player (one `ExoPlayer`, one service); a video replaces the music queue, which comes back paused afterwards; the surface is attached through the `MediaController`** (recommended: notification, buttons, focus, reporting, output profiles for free; the media3 skill forbids a second player) / a second `ExoPlayer` owned by the video screen / a separate video service. ADR 0047.
2. **Video items in the account database as `series`, `season`, `video`, `video_stream` tables, synced with the music in the same full sync; resume positions come from the server** (recommended; Room reads keep the UI offline-first and search unified) / a generic `item` table for every kind / no local copy, server reads per screen. ADR 0048.
3. **Player UI: `media3-ui-compose` `PlayerSurface` (SurfaceView) + `PresentationState` with our own Material 3 Expressive controls, `SubtitleView` wrapped for cues, a full-screen destination on the tab's back stack (not the Now Playing overlay), platform PiP API** (recommended) / `media3-ui-compose-material3` `Player` composable (plain Material, `com.google.android.material` dependency) / `PlayerView` in an `AndroidView` (Google warns about Compose interop on API 34) / Jetpack `core-pip` (alpha). ADR 0049.
4. **Stream selection: `DeviceProfileBuilder.video` from `MediaCodecList` + `Display.getHdrCapabilities`, HLS fMP4 transcodes, subtitle delivery External / Embed / Hls / Encode as in section 5, server-decided through `AudioStreamIndex` / `SubtitleStreamIndex`, downloads as originals only** (recommended) / always ask for direct play and handle failures on the client / converted video downloads through `DownloadHelper` on HLS. ADR 0050.
5. **Video in the Library hub (Movies, Series tiles), Home sections and Search; optional Movies / Series navigation entries** (recommended; ADR 0028 and the owner's Navigation settings cover it) / a separate "Video" tab with its own home.
6. **Movies and episodes share one `video` table** (recommended: one DAO, one offline filter, one FTS kind) / `movie` and `episode` tables.
7. **Auto-play the next episode with a 10 s countdown, on by default; "Next up" through `/Shows/NextUp`** (recommended) / off by default / the next episode from Room order only.
8. **Leaving the player: PiP on the home gesture (auto-enter), pause on Back; "keep playing audio" as an off-by-default setting** (recommended) / always keep playing (like music) / always stop.
9. **No video on TV, no Chromecast, no trailers / extras, no collections (BoxSets), no video genre page, no converted video downloads, no 1.12 bump in Mobile 3** (recommended).
10. **App mode Music / Video / Both as one app-wide DataStore preference, asked in onboarding, changed in Settings and on Home, gating screens through `UiState` and the sync through the repository** (owner requirement; recommended shape) / a per-account mode (an account is one server; the owner uses one phone one way) / a mode derived from the server's libraries (no way to hide video on a mixed server). ADR 0051.
11. **Intro Skipper through Jellyfin's `/MediaSegments` only, no plugin or legacy routes; per-kind skipping settings Off / Show button / Skip automatically** (recommended; the owner's server runs the Jellyfin 12 plugin line, which has no `IntroTimestamps` route) / the plugin's `/Episode/{id}/Segments` route (ties the app to the plugin's DTO).
12. **Per-item and per-season track choice in a `video_track_choice` Room table with indexes and languages, resolved item > season > Settings** (recommended: survives syncs, offline, per account) / in DataStore keyed by id (unbounded keys in a preferences file) / only for the session.

## 10. Decisions (owner, 2026-11-12)

Every recommended option of section 9 is accepted (ADRs 0047-0051 Accepted). The open questions were answered:

1. Library size: under 10,000 video items; full local sync as planned.
2. Subtitle default: **Forced only**.
3. Mobile-data video quality: default **480p**, with the choices on the Video settings page (V7).
4. The owner has HDR10 and Dolby Vision files, some **profile 5**: keep declaring only what the phone reports; the manual tests of V7 name an HDR10, a DV profile 8 and a DV profile 5 file with the expected result for each.
5. Video genres as text only in Mobile 3.
6. Downloads: **season and single episode**, no series download (V10).
7. Audio and subtitle tracks are chosen **in the player and on the movie page, the episode row and the season menu**, remembered per item or season (`video_track_choice`, V5); Settings keeps the default languages.
8. Intro / outro skipping is **in scope and required** (V8): Skip intro / Skip credits from the Intro Skipper segments with their own settings; V8 is no longer optional.
9. New requirement: the **app mode** Music / Video / Both (ADR 0051, V2).
10. Movies and episodes in one `video` table; auto-play on with a 10 s countdown; PiP on the home gesture, pause on Back, keep audio off by default; out of scope confirmed as in section 11.

## 11. Out of scope (Mobile 3)

Video on the TV (TV 2 · Video, including passthrough, frame-rate matching and tunneling), Chromecast, trailers and
extras, collections (`BoxSet`), `MusicVideo`, home videos / photos, live TV, SyncPlay, converted (transcoded) video
downloads, a video genre or people page, playlists of videos, Dolby Vision enhancement-layer (profile 7) handling
beyond what the decoder reports, the Media3 1.12 bump, a second provider's video (Subsonic has none), performance
measurements.
